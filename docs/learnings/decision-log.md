### Brand Identity Direction Set

Decision:

- Explore three identity directions: **Threadline Precision**, **Atelier Cut**, and **Proof Seal**.
- Treat Threadline Precision as the recommended primary direction.
- Treat Atelier Cut as a premium large-format alternate.
- Treat Proof Seal as a limited-use proof/certification extension.

Reason:

- The system needs a primary mark that is minimal, professional, scalable, and Apple-like, while still preserving room for luxury moments and proof-oriented interface cues.

Decision:

- Resolve the NK monogram in one color before applying gold accents or material treatments.

Reason:

- A one-color mark is more robust for favicon, navigation, print, document, and accessibility contexts.
