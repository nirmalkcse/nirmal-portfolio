# North Star

## Vision

Build a premium, cinematic, Apple-like minimal portfolio for Nirmal Kumar that feels less like a resume page and more like a polished professional product experience.

The site should communicate a hybrid identity:

- Data Engineer
- AI / ML Engineer
- Cloud Engineer
- Software Engineer
- Systems thinker

## Internal Creative Direction

**Cinematic Engineering Dossier**

This is an internal build concept only. The public site should not over-explain this phrase. It should simply feel like a refined professional experience where visitors can inspect Nirmal's career, proof, projects, credentials, and future direction.

## Desired Feeling

The portfolio should feel:

- Apple-like in restraint and polish
- Luxurious without looking decorative or loud
- Cinematic without becoming theatrical
- Data-rich without feeling like a dashboard template
- Memorable without using gimmicks
- Professional enough for recruiters, leaders, and senior engineers
- Tactile and oddly satisfying through subtle feedback and motion

## Site Shape

Use a **cinematic homepage plus structured deep-dive pages**.

Homepage:

- Cinematic identity reveal
- Soft role gateway / viewer lens
- Hybrid positioning
- Proof graph preview
- Featured projects
- Timeline preview
- Certification console preview
- Social profile dock
- Resume and contact CTA

Information architecture summary:

- Homepage sequence: hero, role gateway, proof graph preview, featured case files, evidence-backed skill clusters, timeline preview, certification preview, future projects preview, social dock, resume/contact close.
- Primary proof spine: iCORE Enterprise MDM / Entity Resolution Platform, Tier-1 European Telecom Fabric Migration PoC, and BI Migration/Validation/Analytics Delivery.
- Primary role lenses: Recruiter, Engineer, Leader, Explorer.
- Role lenses change emphasis, ordering, and CTA language; they do not block access or create separate siloed sites.
- Core CTAs: View Proof, Download Resume, View Projects, Inspect Credentials, Contact Nirmal.

Deep-dive pages:

- `/projects`
- `/projects/[slug]`
- `/proof`
- `/certifications`
- `/timeline`
- `/profile`
- `/resume`
- `/documents`
- `/future-projects`
- `/contact`

## Primary Audiences

1. Recruiters who need fast role fit, credibility, resume, and contact.
2. Engineers who need technical depth, architecture, code, and tradeoffs.
3. Leaders who need ownership, business relevance, reliability, and outcomes.
4. Explorers who want the richest interactive and personal journey.
